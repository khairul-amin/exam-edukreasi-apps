PRIVACY POLICY - EXAMBRO
========================

Last Updated: March 17, 2026

## 1. INTRODUCTION

EXAMBRO ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") operates the EXAMBRO mobile application.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us

**Account Information:**
- Email address (for Google Sign-In authentication)
- Name (from Google account)
- Profile information (as provided by Google)

**Usage Information:**
- Exam attempt records
- Quiz/test answers and scores
- Time spent on exams
- Login/logout timestamps

**Device Information:**
- Device model and OS version
- Battery status
- Network connection status
- Screen resolution

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**App Analytics:**
- App usage patterns
- Feature interactions
- Error logs and crash reports
- Performance metrics

**Network Data:**
- IP address
- HTTP request logs
- Connection protocol (HTTP/HTTPS)

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for:

- **Authentication & Security:** To secure your account and verify your identity
- **Service Delivery:** To provide exam functionality and features
- **Performance Monitoring:** To improve app stability and performance
- **Analytics:** To understand how you use the app
- **Communication:** To send notifications about exams and updates
- **Compliance:** To meet legal and regulatory requirements
- **Educational Record Keeping:** To maintain exam records as required by law

## 4. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

**Data Storage:**
- Account data stored in Firebase Authentication
- Exam data stored in secure cloud database
- Device credentials stored locally on device
- WebView cache stored locally

**Security Measures:**
- Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication
- HTTPS encryption for network communication
- ProGuard code obfuscation
- No storage of sensitive credentials

**Data Retention:**
- Account data: Until you delete your account
- Exam records: As required by educational institution
- Cache data: Can be cleared manually in app

## 5. OFFLINE MODE

When using the app in offline mode (LAN):
- Some data may be stored locally on your device
- Clearing the app data will remove all cached information
- Original data remains with the educational institution server

## 6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party services:

**Google Services:**
- Google Sign-In for authentication

- Google Play Services

**Your Data with Third Parties:**

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
